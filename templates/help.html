Information on Electricity Prices

Learn about hourly prices, variable prices, taxes, and fees under each respective category below.

Spot Price

The spot price is the price your electricity trading company has to pay for the electricity. This number is often the one shown in various comparisons, newspaper articles, etc.

Spot price does not include any surcharges and VAT.

Hourly / Quarter-hourly Price

With an hourly (or, since October 2025, quarter-hourly) price you pay for your consumption interval by interval. This allows you to use less electricity during expensive periods and more electricity during cheap periods. It's a good choice for those who can move large consumers to times when electricity is cheap.

Variable Monthly Price

The variable monthly price works much like the hourly price, but your consumption is summed up to a monthly total, which is multiplied by the electricity company's variable price determined at the end of the month. It is not a straight average of all prices, but weighted against the consumption of all customers.

Taxes and Fees

On top of the spot price you normally pay a surcharge to your electricity trading company, energy tax, grid fees and VAT. On this page, you can choose between actual price and spot price using the cogwheel in the top right corner.

Electricity Price Calculation

The default setting displays the spot prices of electricity. You can customize how prices are shown:

  1. Click the cogwheel icon in the top right corner.
  2. Enable {{ t("nav.actual_price_instead") }}.
  3. Adjust the parameters:
    • {{ t("customize.multiplier") }}: Used when the electricity company has an added fee in percentage per kilowatt hour. For example, use 1.02 if there's an added fee of 2% per kWh.
    • {{ t("customize.fees") }}: Any fixed addition per kWh, in the chosen currency, before VAT.
    • {{ t("customize.factor") }}: Typically VAT. For instance, use 1.25 for a 25% VAT.

Once you've set your parameters, prices are calculated as:

Price = ((Spot Price × Multiplier) + Fees) × Factor (VAT)

Example: a spot price of 1.00 SEK/kWh, multiplier 1.02, fees 0.10 SEK and factor 1.25 gives ((1.00 × 1.02) + 0.10) × 1.25 = 1.40 SEK/kWh.

All settings are stored in your browser only.

Installing as an app

This site is available as an app (PWA) for Windows, Android and Apple devices.

Windows (Chrome / Edge)

  1. At the right of the address bar, click Install.
  2. Follow the onscreen instructions.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Install, or use the three-dot menu and choose "Install app".
  2. Follow the onscreen instructions.

Apple / iOS (Safari)

  1. Press the "Share" button.
  2. Select "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Add".

Open API

All data is available as JSON, free of charge and without API key:

  • /api/v2/spot?period=daily&area=SE3&startDate=2024-01-01&endDate=2024-01-31&currency=SEK – prices in currency/MWh grouped by hourly (raw intervals), daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or total. Optional interval=PT15M|PT60M selects an exact resolution.
  • /api/v2/hass?area=SE3&currency=SEK – yesterday, today and tomorrow, see Home Assistant.
  • /api/v2/status – data freshness.
